# What's new, newest first, shown in Settings → About eReaderDS.
# "v" lines start a version; every other line is one change, in plain words.
# Add the new version's lines at the top with each release.

v1.16.0
KOReader Sync no longer sends your place without asking: when you open a book, it asks whether to continue from your other device or send your place. Choose Off, Ask when opening or On in Settings → KOReader Sync → Automatic sync.
When your place is already on the sync server, Sync with KOReader says so, and when it was sent.

v1.15.0
KOReader Sync can go one way when you want it to: Settings → KOReader Sync has Send my place (puts your place on the server) and Get my place from the server (goes to where your other device got to, asking first if that's going back).
Sync with KOReader on the first page of Settings still works out which way to go by itself.

v1.14.0
KOReader Sync now finds your place from any KOReader or CrossPoint device, whichever way it matches books (by file name or by file contents), and sends yours so both kinds can find it.
Sync messages tell you more: the chapter and percentage, and where your other device was.
Books from Standard Ebooks no longer show little boxes next to dashes.

v1.13.1
KOReader Sync remembers a place you've already jumped to (or chose to stay away from), even after restarting, so it isn't offered again. A newer one from your other device still is.
If you read on while offline, your place is sent the next time you open the book.

v1.13.0
The on-screen keyboard: shift now capitalizes just the next letter (press it twice for caps lock), and you can move around in what you've typed: tap the text, or press up to it and use left and right.
Typing a server address, keys for https://, http://, www. and .com save a trip to the symbols.
The keyboard's instructions now match each field, and logging out of KOReader Sync asks first.

v1.12.0
Settings is laid out around what you need while reading: your book (contents, bookmarks, find, jump) and how the page looks (brightness first, then text size, theme and font) on the first page; things you set once on the second.
If you use KOReader Sync, "Sync with KOReader" is now on the first page of Settings.
Night Theme is now called Night Mode.
Your own sync server has a name, and you can change its address, rename it or delete it. The server list opens as its own page.
Typing a password, tap the eye to see what you've typed. The shift key is now an arrow.

v1.11.0
Fourteen new themes, 25 in all. Dark: Dark Sepia, Gruvbox, Nord, Solarized Dark, Dracula, Catppuccin, Everforest, and Red Night, red on black for reading in bed without losing your night vision.
Light: Gruvbox Light, Catppuccin Latte, Rosé Pine Dawn, Parchment, Sky (a pale blue many readers find easier than white), and Mint, the pale green page of the Kindle app.
Books that draw a few special letters as tiny pictures now keep them inside the words, instead of each one breaking the line.

v1.10.1
Fixed a crash when tapping the My Books options card, and a damaged book or dictionary can no longer crash the app.
Your place, bookmarks and settings are safe even when the SD card is full, and two copies of the same book keep separate highlight files.
Quitting and closing Send Books no longer freeze while something is downloading or a phone is connected.
Held the other way round, question cards and messages now appear on the touchscreen where you can tap them.
Books look better: long web addresses and Chinese or Japanese text wrap properly, poems keep their blank lines, and plain text files read correctly.
Find in Book carries on where it stopped, and many smaller fixes and speed-ups throughout.

v1.10.0
Hold it the other way round: in Settings → Reading & Device, set "Hold it with buttons" to "On the left" and everything turns around, for holding it with the buttons under your left hand.
Held that way, the A, B, X and Y buttons work like a second D-pad: they turn pages while reading, and move and select in menus.

v1.9.3
Tap Theme in Settings to see every theme, with your book shown in each one before you choose.

v1.9.2
Drawings in books, like chapter numbers and ornaments, now sit on the page in the text's colour instead of in a white box, on every theme.

v1.9.1
KOReader Sync: "Sync this book now" goes to where your other device is, even when it's only a page or two ahead.

v1.9.0
KOReader Sync: carry your place in a book between eReaderDS and KOReader or CrossPoint (a phone, Kobo, Kindle, Xteink X4...). Set it up in Settings → KOReader Sync.
When you open a book another device has read further, it asks if you want to jump there.
The on-screen keyboard has capitals and symbols.

v1.8.0
Put it down and it saves the battery: after 10 minutes with nothing pressed, the screens dim, then turn off. Press any button or touch the screen to bring them back. Change the time, or turn it off, in Settings → Reading & Device.

v1.7.0
Your highlights and bookmarks are also saved as a file for each book, in the Highlights folder on your SD card, to read on a computer.
Bookmarks and Highlights can show everything, just highlights or just bookmarks: press left or right, or tap the label at the top.

v1.6.0
Books are marked finished when you reach the last page. My Books shows which you're reading and which you've finished.
Press Y on a book in My Books to mark it finished (or not), hide finished books, or delete it.
Sorting by progress groups your books under Reading, Not Started and Finished.
A new Series order puts each series together, in order: book 1, book 2, and so on.

v1.5.0
My Books finds books in folders inside the Ebook folder, so you can copy a whole Calibre library or keep your own folders.
Books are listed by the title and author inside each book, not the file name, and sorting by author uses last names.
Every screen is tidier: button hints look the same everywhere and sit on the touchscreen, and deleting anything asks the same way.
My Books has its Get Books button beside the title, and shows more books at once.

v1.4.0
Lists scroll with a finger: slide up or down on My Books, Get books, Table of Contents, Bookmarks and Highlights, Find in book and Fonts. On the book page, sliding still changes the brightness.
Table of Contents, Bookmarks and Highlights, and Find in book are now one list on the touchscreen. Tap an entry to see it in full on the top screen, and tap it again to go there.
Table of Contents shows where each chapter is in the book, and where you are.
Find in book shows the whole paragraph around each match.

v1.3.1
Standard Ebooks now has its whole collection: browse the newest books, or by subject, collection or author, or search.
Big catalog pages load without freezing the screen.

v1.3.0
Send books from your phone or computer over Wi-Fi: in Get books, choose Send from your phone or computer, then scan the code or open the address it shows.
Fonts can be sent the same way.

v1.2.1
In Settings, tap "Swipe for more options" to go to the next page.
Tapping ‹ or › in Settings is more reliable.
If your chosen font is removed from the SD card, the book switches to Crimson Pro.
Small fixes to make the app steadier.

v1.2.0
Crimson Pro is built in, and is the font new readers start with.
The Fonts page has its Get more fonts button beside the title.
Get more fonts is in alphabetical order, and can show just serif or sans-serif fonts.

v1.1.0
Settings has bigger, easier-to-tap rows on two pages: swipe for more. Tap ‹ or › to change a value.
