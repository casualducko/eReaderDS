# What's new, newest first, shown in Settings → Page turns & device.
# "v" lines start a version; every other line is one change, in plain words.
# Add the new version's lines at the top with each release.

v0.3.14
The version number in Settings is easier to read.

v0.3.13
Tapping anywhere else closes the update message, instead of turning the page by accident.

v0.3.12
Swipe to turn the pages of What's new.
The newest version's notes stand out, so you can see what just changed.

v0.3.11
What's new: see what changed in each version, in Settings → Page turns & device.
After an update, a note offers to show you what's new.
Messages now match your theme instead of appearing in a black box.

v0.3.10
Tap the "Update available" message to go straight to the update.
Settings shows Update in bold while a new version is waiting.

v0.3.9
Updates over Wi-Fi: eReaderDS tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you. Your books and reading progress stay put.
The library and Get books lists are on the touchscreen now: tap a book once to see it, tap again to open it.
A new font page shows every font with a sample page, and can show just serif or sans-serif fonts.
Seven new fonts: EB Garamond, Lora, Merriweather, PT Serif, Spectral, Vollkorn and Andika.
Project Gutenberg is fast again, and no longer lists links that go nowhere.
Find in book results are easier to scan.

v0.3.8
Find in book: search for any word or phrase and jump straight to it.
Search Project Gutenberg or your Calibre library by title or author.
A touchscreen keyboard for both, or use the D-pad.

v0.3.7
More reliable on ROCKNIX.
Help matches your Tap setting.

v0.3.6
Tap a chapter in Contents, or a bookmark, to jump to it.
New readers start at a comfortable brightness.

v0.3.5
The menu icon puts itself in place on the stock firmware.

v0.3.4
A Help page with all the buttons and touch gestures.
Clearer messages about where your books go.

v0.3.3
A new icon in the Ports menu.
Book covers stay in color while you browse, even on the E-ink theme.
Friendlier settings for new readers: Sepia, bigger text and a clock.

v0.3.2
eReaderDS runs on ROCKNIX too.
The clock shows the right time.

v0.3.1
Pinch to make the text bigger or smaller.
Tap the top edge to hide or show the status bars.

v0.3.0
Download books over Wi-Fi from Project Gutenberg or your own Calibre library.
Look up words, read footnotes, bookmark pages and jump anywhere in a book.
