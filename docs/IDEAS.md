# Feature ideas

Drawn from comparing eReaderDS with KOReader, Kindle, Kobo, Apple Books, Google
Play Books, Moon+ Reader, ReadEra, PocketBook and CrossPoint (researched
2026-09-26). Grouped by value for effort; nothing here is built yet.

## Worth doing next (high value, small)

1. **Bookmarks.** Press a button (e.g. Y long-press or a menu row) to bookmark
   the spread, and list bookmarks alongside Contents. Every reader has it.
2. **Time left.** "12 min left in chapter · 4 h 10 min in book", learned from
   your own page-turn speed. A new Status bar option. Kindle and KOReader
   users rate it highly.
3. **Go back after a jump.** After jumping via Contents or Jump to %, one
   press returns to where you were. Cheap to build and prevents losing your
   place.
4. **Hyphenation.** Pattern-based word breaking (small pattern files, as in
   KOReader and CrossPoint) removes the wide gaps justified text sometimes
   gets. English first.
5. **Library sorting.** Sort by recent, title, author or progress, with
   recently read first by default. Matters once there are more than a handful
   of books.

## Good fits for this device (medium)

6. **Footnotes on the other page.** Selecting a footnote link shows the note
   on the opposite screen, so the text never moves. This uses the dual screen
   better than any single-screen reader can.
7. **Dictionary on the other page.** An offline StarDict dictionary copied to
   the SD card; tapping a word shows its definition on the facing page.
   CrossPoint does this on far weaker hardware.
8. **Sleep screen.** Show the book's cover (or the current page, dimmed)
   briefly when the lid closes, then turn the screens off.
9. **Button remapping.** Choose what L/R, L2/R2, Y and the stick do (for
   example, L2/R2 turning pages instead of jumping chapters).
10. **Reading statistics.** Time read today and this week, and pages per
    session, shown on the About page or in the library. No charts.

## Maybe later (bigger or niche)

11. **Highlights.** Drag on the touchscreen to highlight, then list them per
    book and export them to a text file on the SD card. Free-text notes would
    need an on-screen keyboard, so they're left out.
12. **Search in book.** Needs text entry. A lighter version: "find next
    occurrence of this word" from a tapped word.
13. **Auto page turn.** Turn pages on a timer. Rarely used.
14. **Collections.** Group books automatically by author or series, or by
    folders inside `Ebook`, since there's no keyboard for naming.

## Skip (bloat or needs internet)

Text-to-speech, X-Ray / Word Wise, translation and Wikipedia lookup, cloud
sync and OPDS catalogs, reading goals and charts, and page-thumbnail browsing.
Each is heavy for 1 GB of RAM, needs a network, or is rarely used.
